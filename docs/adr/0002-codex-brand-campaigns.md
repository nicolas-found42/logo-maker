# 0002: A branding campaign owns its evidence and delivery

Status: accepted, 2026-11-13. Supersedes the contour-only identity rule in ADR 0001.

## Context

Found42 delivered complete modeled objects and adapted lettering. Its local runner supplied
sampling controls, inspection cameras, render reuse and delivery verification. The reusable tool
must own those operations. A valid archive, owner acceptance and visual qualification answer
different questions.

## Decision

Codex on Apple Silicon runs a persistent campaign. Research precedes ideation. The owner decides
the brief, selected concepts and final acceptance, unless they explicitly delegate a decision.
Each concept may use custom geometry in a self-contained packed Blender scene. The scene is the
render input; construction scripts and documented inputs remain editable handoff sources. Flat
and compact companions preserve recognizable identity, with exact source artwork retained for
adaptations. They need not share every contour with the physical object.

The campaign owns budgets, three meaningful repair rounds per concept, reference coverage,
immutable revisions, render reuse, review currency and delivery. It uses 8192 samples, adaptive
0.01 and automatic minimum; numerical settings alone never establish qualification. Each design
needs 40 distinct photographic captures and matched craft/independent holdout evidence for its
critical construction features. A holdout used to inform a repair becomes development evidence.

Packed-scene qualification binds a verified dependency inventory, exact renderer/runtime,
sampling settings, retained positive/degraded/held-out comparisons and explicit owner review.
Required failed or unsettled findings keep production delivery in review-needed state. Owner
acceptance is separately recorded. Older declarative qualification remains unchanged.

The client handoff excludes private research. The private evidence archive binds the handoff,
review and execution bytes. Only reference copies with recorded redistribution permission may
enter a client package. Codex skill, CLI and MCP must come from the same installed revision.

## Consequences

Production qualification requires fresh measurements after render-affecting changes. Rendering
and reviewing can continue as accurately labeled studies while qualification is pending. Release
validation uses three frozen unfamiliar briefs in fresh Codex sessions, including interruption
and recovery. Found42 remains a regression case. Claude and OMP compatibility is deferred.
